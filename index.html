ABOUT MERIDIAN

Built for people
who look up

We started Meridian after a decade of watching beautiful telescopes go back in their boxes, defeated by finder-scopes and star charts. The M1 strips that away. It's one optical tube, one mount, one job — turning a patch of dark sky into something you can actually see.

Every M1 is collimated and star-tested by hand in our optics shop before it ships, then logged in a runbook that ships in the box with your unit's exact serial and test night.
